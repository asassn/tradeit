# Pre-registration — the 250-session rank as an exclusion gate, 2020–2024

Committed before any 2020s run. The idea comes from a **diagnostic** in §19:
compounded over 2010–2019 the bottom quintile of `pct_250` ran at −31.6%/yr
against −7.2%/yr for the top, a 24-point gap holding in both halves, while the
top quintile was roughly the universe average. That says the information is in
*avoidance*. A diagnostic cannot be its own evidence, so this is the test.

**A gate is a portfolio claim, not a signal claim**, so it is tested the way a
portfolio would feel it: the same strategy, the same universe, the same costs,
run twice — once refusing the bottom quintile, once not. Paired, so the only
difference is the gate.

```
THE GATE
  On the session a candidate is decided, refuse it if its pct_250 percentile
  is below 0.20 -- the bottom quintile, as §19 measured it, ranked by
  RelativeStrengthEngine.rank_cross_section across the securities of that
  sample holding a served bar that day, benchmark QQQ.
  Entry only. A gate refuses; it can never promote, size, or exit -- the same
  rule §11 set for breakout_confirmation.
  THE THRESHOLD IS FIXED AT 0.20 AND IS NOT TUNED. If it fails, no other
  threshold is tried on this data.
  A candidate whose percentile is UNKNOWN -- too little history, or fewer
  than min_universe_for_rank peers that day -- is NOT refused. The gate does
  not apply to it, exactly as §11's breakout gate reports NOT_APPLICABLE
  rather than blocking. Both counts are reported. Decided here, before the
  run, because "no rank" and "a bad rank" are different facts.

PERIOD    2020-01-02 .. 2024-12-31. Trailing history from 2019-01-01 for the
          250-session warm-up only.

DISCLOSURE: THIS PERIOD IS NOT UNREAD.
  The relative_volume_20 out-of-sample study and the volatility-sizing
  backtest both ran over 2010-2024. No relative-strength result has been read
  on it, and no gate has been tested on it, but it is not virgin data and is
  not described as such. It is also the COVID crash and the 2021 retail
  rally: a regime a momentum-exclusion rule may behave unusually in, stated
  before the run rather than after.

UNIVERSE  Four disjoint samples, backtest_survivorship._arms, cap 300:
          300 survived + 300 died each (population 3,898 survived, 1,245
          died; cap 400 would not be disjoint, cap 300 is -- verified).

STRATEGY  The MovingAverageCross baseline exactly as §15-§17 ran it: fast 50,
          slow 200, 8% stop, 2% participation, delisting after 10 silent
          sessions, 100,000 initial capital.

RECOVERY  Delisted holdings at 1.0 and again at 0.0. §16's per-security
          classification covers the 2000s population only, so the 2020s runs
          use the scalar. The comparison is paired, so recovery affects both
          arms alike; requiring the result under both is stricter than either.

THE GATE PASSES ONLY IF ALL THREE HOLD, UNDER BOTH RECOVERY VALUES
  1. Gated total return exceeds ungated in at least 3 of the 4 samples.
  2. The mean CAGR improvement across the four samples is positive.
  3. The mean maximum drawdown is not worse by more than 1.0 percentage
     point -- a gate may not buy return with risk.
  Sharpe, trade count and exposure are reported and are not criteria.

WHAT DOES NOT COUNT
  An improvement in one sample; a return gain paid for with drawdown; a
  result that holds at one recovery value and not the other.

WHAT A PASS WOULD MEAN
  That excluding the weakest fifth by twelve-month relative strength helped
  this rule, in this period, on a survivorship-honest universe -- grounds to
  propose the gate, not to enable it. Live trading stays behind its interlock,
  and the corpus gate still reads SURVIVOR_BIASED.

TRIALS  1 (the gate). Ledger 28 -> 29; hurdle at 29 trials measured 2.059,
        recorded for the ledger though these criteria are portfolio-level
        rather than t-based.
```
