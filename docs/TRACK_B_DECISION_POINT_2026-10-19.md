# Track B has reached its decision point with 0 of 20 trials spent

Written 2026-10-19, after keeping the combination line closed and then checking
what Track B actually had left. **The answer is nothing that does not need a
decision from the owner**, and that is a result rather than a stall.

## The budget was never the constraint

The delivery plan gave Track B **20 registered trials, 144 → 164**, and a
stopping rule: *if 20 further registered trials produce no out-of-sample
survivor, the recorded conclusion is that this corpus does not support a
pattern-based edge at the effect sizes measurable in it.*

**Zero of those 20 have been spent.** The ledger still reads 144. The options
ran out before the budget did, which is a different and better-evidenced place
to arrive: the stopping rule's conclusion is now supported by *measurement of
what is resolvable* rather than by a tally of failures.

## Every item on Track B's ordered list is resolved

The plan named three, in order.

**1. The portfolio-level question — checked and withdrawn.**
`PATTERN_PROGRAM_POWER_2026-10-16.md`'s first addendum computed it rather than
estimating it. Diversification worked exactly as predicted: seven setups
correlating at a mean 0.17 gave 3.5 effective independent cells and cut block sd
from 0.96% to 0.52%, moving the detectable effect to 0.36%. **Then the signs
cancelled** — the pennant's −0.752% and the bull flag's −0.125% against the
positives — and the honest equal-weighted combination came to **+0.318% against
a 0.36% limit, t +2.66 against a 2.99 hurdle. It fails in sample.** There was
nothing to register.

That addendum also recorded the trap at its exact size: selecting each setup's
sign from the same data gives **+0.496%, t +3.93**, comfortably past the hurdle
and not a legitimate test. Written down precisely so nobody re-derives it later
as a finding.

**2. The extended Stage A window — done, and it did not reopen the program.**
2003–2019 doubled the blocks as projected, 20 → 34. The detection limit did not
halve, because the added years contain 2008 and median block sd rose 1.00% →
1.40%. **Years of history needed to resolve 0.50%: 18 became 35.** Two setups
improved, two worsened, the effects themselves shrank, and **no setup's effect
now exceeds its own detection limit.**

**3. Items 6–40, conditional on the extended window showing an effect near 1%.**
**The condition was not met.** The largest effect on the extended window is
+0.76% against a 1.36% limit. The trigger for item 3 never fired, so it is not
pending — it is excluded by its own condition.

## What the constraint turned out to be

Not the number of blocks, and not the placebo — which works, at correlations
0.91–0.99 removing 54–83% of market variance. It is that **effects of 0.1–0.8%
sit below what block variance of 1.0–2.6% can resolve at any window length this
corpus can offer.**

## And §58 does not change it

The constraint re-measurement was worth doing and it is banked: §46 reproduced
to two decimals a month later, and the diversification limit costs the archive
nothing (mean +0.04% across 64 arms). **But it produces no candidate.** A
constraint that leaves results unchanged cannot manufacture an effect that was
not there, and the combination line it would have been tested on is closed by
§48's registration.

## The three options that remain, and what each actually costs

Named in the power analysis and unchanged by anything since:

**A. Shorter horizons.** The atlas already says the edge is not there. This is
the cheapest option and the one with the least reason behind it: it would spend
trials searching where the descriptive work already looked.

**B. Portfolio construction on signals rather than patterns.** Narrower than it
sounds. §46/§48 *was* this — low volatility × earnings surprise as a portfolio —
and §48's registration closed not only that pair but **"no other pair drawn from
the measured set."** So this option requires a signal that is **not yet
measured**, which means building and measuring one first. Free in money, real in
time, and with no prior reason to expect a new signal to clear a bar the
measured ones did not.

**C. More history.** *"Only the last changes the arithmetic"* — the power
analysis's own words. The constraint is resolvable effect size, and only more
independent blocks move it. The corpus reaches 1990 **for survivors only**, so
this means purchased point-in-time history, and it is the one option that costs
money.

## What this document is for

**The stopping rule said this moment is a decision point, not an open loop**, and
that it would "redirect Track B to signals, to fundamentals, or to purchased
history, each a separate proposition." It also said it would **not** stop or
delay Track A — and it has not: Track A is four phases closed with only Phase
12's owed thresholds outstanding.

So Track B is **paused at a decision, not blocked by a problem.** Nothing
further should be registered until that decision is made, because registering
on either of the free options would spend ledger budget against a known-negative
prior — and the ledger's whole purpose is that trials spent carelessly raise the
hurdle for everything after them.
