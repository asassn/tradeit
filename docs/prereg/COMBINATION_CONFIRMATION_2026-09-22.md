# Pre-registration — the combination on the held-out window, 2020–2025

Committed **before the combination was run on any session after 2019-12-31**.
Licensed by `COMBINATION_PORTFOLIO_2026-09-22`, which granted exactly one
confirmation registration and required it to disclose what follows.

## What is held out, and what is not

**The combination has never been run on 2020–2025.** Its ingredients have:

| | measured on 2020–2025 | where |
|---|---|---|
| low volatility, as a signal | yes — it passed there | §35 |
| low volatility, as a portfolio | yes — it lost to random by 4.35 pp/yr | §37 |
| earnings surprise | yes — 2013–2025 pooled, this window included | §40, §41 |

So this is **out of sample for the interaction and in sample for both of its
parts**. That is the strongest confirmation this corpus can still offer, and it
is weaker than a clean one. Recorded here so the result is read at its true
weight rather than a flattering one.

## What §46 found, in full

2013–2019, four samples, everything the platform's own. At recovery 1.0:
combined **5.74%** against random 3.19%, calm 3.07%, surprise 3.31% — **+2.54 pp
over random in 4 of 4 samples**, +2.67 over calm, +2.43 over surprise, drawdown
9.5% against 12.9%, and **+2.49 pp under stress costs**. At recovery 0.0 it
failed: −0.77 pp against random, behind its own surprise component. The
registration required both, so §46 is a **fail** carrying a live interaction.
