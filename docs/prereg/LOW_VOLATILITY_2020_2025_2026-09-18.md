# Pre-registration — low volatility among tradeable securities, 2020–2025

Committed **before `realized_volatility_60` was computed on any session after
2019-12-31**, and before any scan of 2020–2025 was run. The git timestamp is the
evidence. **Re-opened by the owner's decision of 2026-09-18** ("reopen"), which
reverses §27's written closure of the volatility line for this one test only.

**Why it is re-opened.** §27 closed the line on the finding that the §26 effect
"was describing the corpus's coverage gaps rather than the market", and §33
claimed to confirm that: above $1M/day the effect read t −1.89. Both rested on
dollar volume read through the defect in `RESEARCH_01_DATA_DICTIONARY.md` §0.9 —
stored volume already restated for later splits and restated again on read — which
let future winners into a floored sample and kept future distressed names out.
Re-read through the corrected volume (`67894dd`) on the common grid, §34 measured
t −4.34 above $1M/day and t −3.31 above $10M/day. That was the ninth reading of
2010–2019 for volatility and confers nothing. This is the test that can.

```
PRE-REGISTRATION -- low volatility among tradeable securities, 2020-2025
written 2026-09-18, BEFORE realized_volatility_60 was computed on any
session after 2019-12-31 and before any 2020-2025 scan was run

HYPOTHESIS
  Among securities trading at least $1,000,000 a day on a date, those
  with lower 60-session realized volatility earn higher 63-session
  forward returns.
  Direction DECLARED NEGATIVE -- the sign §26 registered and every
  reading since has shown. Not re-derived.

DATA -- no volatility study has read it
  2020-01-02 .. 2025-12-31. Every outcome ends on or before 2025-12-31.
  Grid: 289 dates, 2020-01-02 .. 2025-09-25, last outcome 2025-12-24.

UNIVERSE
  security_spans_2026-09-18.csv (built before this registration, from the
  corpus as it stands): every security with a raw print overlapping the
  window. No whole-life bar filter; 100 of the security's own bars
  required point-in-time, up to and including the signal session.

SAMPLING -- tradeit.signals.sampling, the common calendar grid
  Every 5th XNYS session; horizon 63; every security sampled on the same
  sessions and every outcome on a date measured to the same session.

INCLUSION ON A GRID DATE -- all point-in-time
  Both endpoints priced above zero and traded (volume > 0).
  atr_percent_14 <= 1.0 (DATA_DICTIONARY §0.8).
  20-session average dollar volume >= $1,000,000, on the corrected read
  (67894dd). Observations whose turnover window holds an UNDETERMINED
  volume basis are excluded and counted; if they exceed 5% of the
  would-be-floored sample, the hypothesis must pass all four criteria
  with them excluded AND included (the rule from ADX_CONFIRMATION
  Amendment 1, unchanged).

ESTIMATOR
  tradeit.signals.cross_section.cross_sectional_ic as committed at
  9b790e1: per-date Spearman IC on dates with >= 20 securities, mean over
  non-overlapping 63-session calendar blocks, t from the block means with
  Newey-West lag 1.

HURDLE
  Ledger 100 -> 101. |t| > 2.5341 = expected_max_of_normals(101),
  measured. One trial: one signal, one horizon, one floor.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT COUNTS AS A PASS -- ALL FOUR

  1 Block t < -2.5341, IC negative.

  2 The least-volatile quintile compounds ahead of its date's universe.
    On each date: the GEOMETRIC mean 63-session return of the bottom
    realized-volatility quintile minus the geometric mean of every
    security on that date. Positive when averaged over calendar blocks,
    AND positive at the median across dates.
    Geometric, not arithmetic, for §26's reason (SIGNAL_RESEARCH_01 §7):
    sorting on volatility sorts on the variance of the thing being
    averaged, and in sample the arithmetic and median spreads disagreed in
    sign. §26's criterion 1 was geometric; this is its cross-sectional form.

  3 IC negative in both halves, 2020-2022 and 2023-2025.

  4 IC negative in at least 4 of 5 PRICE quintiles assigned within each
    date, on the raw share price at the signal session.
    Price, not volatility, because §26 and §27 found the effect "mostly a
    price effect" -- conditioning volatility on itself would test nothing.
    A pass here says low volatility predicts within each price band, which
    is the claim §27 said failed.

WHAT A PASS WOULD LICENSE
  A SCOPED PROPOSITION for low volatility as a candidate factor in
  strategy work -- the first signal on this corpus to pass a registered,
  out-of-sample, tradeable-universe test at an honest standard error. It
  moves no weight, gate or strategy parameter by itself.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
THE MACHINERY CHECK

  CALIBRATION -- realized_volatility_60 shuffled WITHIN each date 200
  times (seed 20260918), block t recomputed each time. If the 95th
  percentile of |t| exceeds 2.3 the standard error is too narrow on this
  panel and the run is VOID. Charged nothing: it tests no hypothesis.

  NO POSITIVE CONTROL THIS TIME, and why. A positive control needs an
  effect already known to be present in the window, and nothing has been
  measured in 2020-2025. The only candidate would be this effect itself,
  which is circular. The machinery -- grid, scan, read and estimator --
  passed its positive control unchanged on 2010-2019 in §34 (t -8.89),
  and the calibration above checks the one part that could behave
  differently on new data.

DIAGNOSTICS, DECLARED NOW, DECIDING NOTHING
  The same IC with no floor, and above $10,000,000/day. Reported so the
  result can be read in context; neither can confer a pass, and neither
  may be substituted for the registered floor after the fact.

--------------------------------------------------------------------
RESOLUTION -- predicted, so a null is not over-read

  From §34's above-floor reading (IC -0.0840, t -4.34 on 40 blocks) the
  block standard error is ~0.0194. Six years hold ~24 blocks, so it
  scales to ~0.0250, and the smallest detectable IC is ~0.063.
  §34's effect was 0.084 -- detectable if it persists at that size. An
  effect of 0.06 or less would NOT be resolved by this test.

STOP RULE -- declared now
  If this fails any criterion, the low-volatility line is CLOSED on this
  corpus, and stays closed: no other window, floor, horizon, lookback or
  volatility definition is tried. §27's closure is restored with this
  result as its reason. That holds even if the failure is for resolution
  -- an effect too small to see in six years of fresh data on thousands
  of securities is too small to build on.

TRIALS  1. Ledger 100 -> 101.
```
